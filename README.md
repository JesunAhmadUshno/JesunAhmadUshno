## Jesun Ahmad Ushno

**AI Engineer and Data Architect.** I build tools that do serious work without
sending your data anywhere.

Most of what I ship runs entirely in the browser: media conversion through
FFmpeg.wasm, statistical analysis through Pyodide, peer-to-peer transfer over
WebRTC. No upload step, no server-side copy, no retention policy to trust. That
constraint comes from four years of IT advisory and ISO 27001 audit work, where
the cheapest way to secure data was to never collect it.

Currently at [Gallea Ai](https://galleaai.com) (Toronto). Previously KPMG IT
Advisory, Officer through Consultant.
